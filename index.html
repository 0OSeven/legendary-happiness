If something goes wrong

Nothing happens when I pick a video. Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android can both read iPhone recordings. Desktop Chrome and Firefox often cannot decode HEIC/HEVC .mov files, so use a phone or convert first.

The export has no sound. Not every browser lets a page capture audio from a playing video. The picture still stabilizes; the clip just comes out silent.

Saving on iPhone. Tap Save video, then use the share sheet to put it in Photos. Long-pressing the player also works.

It looks worse, not better. Very dark footage, heavy motion blur, or a subject filling the whole frame all confuse the motion tracking. Try more crop allowance, or turn off tilt correction.

Export takes as long as the clip. That is expected — the encoder records in real time. Analysis runs faster than real time; the export does not.

How it works

Every frame is shrunk to a thumbnail and compared with the one before it to work out which way the camera moved. Adding those up gives the camera's actual path, jitter included. That path gets averaged over the smoothing window to produce the path a gimbal would have taken, and each frame is shifted and turned by the difference, then zoomed slightly so no empty edges show.

It corrects shift and roll. It does not correct perspective wobble or rolling-shutter jello, which need the phone's gyroscope — something a web page cannot read.
